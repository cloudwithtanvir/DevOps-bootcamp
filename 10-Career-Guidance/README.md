## Suggested certification path (one at a time)

1. AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (optional for beginners), then AWS Solutions Architect – Associate
2. HashiCorp Terraform Associate
3. Certified Kubernetes Administrator (CKA), or KCNA for beginners
4. Later, depending on your local job market: Azure Administrator (AZ-104) or Google Associate Cloud Engineer

## Job search abroad

- Check visa and work-permit rules before targeting roles.
- Good entry routes: cloud support engineer, junior DevOps, NOC/operations, or an internal move from IT support or development.
- Use referrals: the Bangladeshi diaspora in tech is a strong network, and alumni of this program refer future batches.
- Tailor each application to the stack in the job description and point to the matching part of your capstone.
